# AI tools in interviews: policy timeline, 2024 → Oct 2026

Staged by the Data: Companies agent on 2026-10-11 as input for the hub's "then vs now" section.
Sources: `docs/ai-interviews/research/01-*.md` (sections 1–3, 5, 8), `02-*.md`, `04-*.md` (section A), plus
three official pages re-checked on 2026-10-11 (Canva, Zapier, Perplexity). Every source here is also in
`companies.sources.yaml`.

**How to read this**
- Dates are when the event happened or was reported. Tags: **[official]** company page, **[news]** press or
  newsletter, **[first-hand]** a candidate's own account, **[secondary]** prep site, **[snippet]** the page was
  blocked and only a search snippet was seen.
- Each entry is a report, not a verified fact. Where a company's own page and a prep site disagree, the company's
  page wins.
- **Context** entries are not interview rules (employee reviews, cheating tools, market data). They explain why
  the rules changed.

**Corrections and why**
1. **AI tools row.** Added Perplexity's official *required* Agentic Coding round, the most direct example of a
   required-AI round, which the research table left out. Added the first-hand Stripe and aggregator Glean
   AI-coding rounds. Noted that Anthropic's ban has a documented exception: the performance take-home allows AI
   (official, 2026-01-21).
2. **Interview location.** The original said Google "requires at least one in-person round". The sources support
   only that Pichai *said* Google would add one, a pilot finished, and a rollout was planned. Research 04 could
   not confirm a company-wide rule. A first-hand Oct 2026 PracHub report shows in-person onsites still happen.
3. **Work trials, 2023–2024.** Removed "Automattic". No source in the research cites it. Linear's trial week is
   sourced: in Sep 2025 it was described as "unchanged", which implies it existed before.
4. **AI-specific design.** Marked FDE decomposition rounds as prep-site only. Research 01 §4 found no first-hand
   confirmation.
5. **Senior/Staff signal.** The original listed the Google pilot as a Senior/Staff AI-fluency criterion. The pilot
   is reported for junior and mid-level roles only. Also separated interview criteria (Zapier) from employee
   performance reviews (Meta, Shopify).
6. **Titles.** "Coined" is now "popularized", matching research 01 §6. Removed the quoted phrase "hottest role
   of 2025", which has no source in the research, and replaced it with the sourced FT/Indeed figure. Marked the
   LinkedIn ranking as seen only via secondary coverage.

**Not changed, but check before publishing**
- The Meta AI-round scope (E7/M2 vs E7+/M1) is still a conflict between prep sites. Neither is Meta documentation.
- "Shopify", "DoorDash" and "Rippling" in the AI-tools row rest on a secondary page, a search snippet and an
  undated Blind post, in that order.
- Cursor's onsite: the CEO says two days in the office. Prep sites and a first-hand snippet say one 8–9 hour day.
  Only prep sites say the onsite is paid and allows AI.
